Approach

How the work gets done

Small agencies can't afford fragile software. Each system here is built to be understood, audited and handed off, and to keep working when its author is out of the office.

  • Policy as dataRules, blueprints and rubrics live in versioned JSON or YAML, so they change without a rebuild.
  • Reversible by defaultEvery automated change is journaled with a one-click undo, and baselines are locked before anything is scored.
  • Built for real constraintsLocked-down workstations, blocked add-ins and carrier portals with no API are treated as design inputs, not blockers.
  • Verified before shippedAutomated tests, deploy records and documented acceptance checks come with every release.
